# Tells: what makes a page read as machine-made

Each tell is a default that appears because it is the most common choice, not because it fits. Treat a tell as a question: is it here for a reason I can say in one sentence? If yes, keep it. If not, replace it with what the inventory offers. `check.mjs` measures the ones marked (✓).

## Layout
- **The template order.** Hero, logo strip, three feature cards, testimonial, pricing, FAQ, CTA. Instead, order sections by the argument (claim → proof).
- **Identical cards with an icon on top.** Instead, vary size by importance, use real screenshots or pictures, or make it a list or a table.
- **A centred hero with a pill badge, a gradient headline word and two buttons.** Instead, start from the direction's composition.
- **Everything boxed** in 1px rounded cards. Instead, group with space and alignment, and use surfaces only where a thing really is an object (a document, a panel).
- **Empty screens** (✓) that are just padding. Darkness or white space must hold something: a word, a picture, a pause on purpose.

## Type
- **The default family** (Inter, Roboto, Poppins, Montserrat) with no reason. Instead, take the sheet's pairing.
- **Three or more families**, many sizes (✓), and grey body text under 16px (✓).
- **Headlines over two lines** (✓) and one-word last lines. Shorten the copy before you shrink the font.
- **A coloured or italic accent word** in every headline. Use it once per page at most.
- **Tiny uppercase tracked labels** above every heading ("FEATURES", "WHY US"). Use them only where they carry information (a form field, a figure number).

## Colour
- **Purple-to-blue gradients**, and violet or indigo as "tech". Take the colour from the brand or the sheet.
- **Cream and terracotta everywhere** (the 2024–26 "warm AI" look). Fine with a reason, not as a default.
- **Glows, neon shadows and glassmorphism** on resting elements.
- **Low contrast** (✓) on coloured or dark sections.

## Pictures
- **Stock clichés:** handshakes, laptops on desks, people pointing at screens, glowing brains, abstract 3D blobs.
- **Illustrated dashboards** instead of the real product. Show the real UI or record it.
- **Mismatched light and style** between pictures. Pick one treatment.
- **No pictures at all** (✓) on a page about physical things.

## Words
- **Slop words** (✓): unlock, unleash, seamless, elevate, supercharge, revolutionize, game-changer, cutting-edge, next-level, delve, empower, effortless, leverage, world-class, reimagine.
- **Generic buttons** (✓): Get started, Learn more, Click here, Submit, Discover more. Name the action and the object instead.
- **Vague claims:** "the future of X", "built for teams of all sizes". Use a number, a name or a fact from the inventory.
- **Invented proof:** made-up quotes, logos, "10,000+ users", star ratings. Never use them. Mark sample content.
- **Em-dash rhythm** (✓) and triplets ("fast, simple, powerful") in every paragraph.
- **Filler** (✓): lorem ipsum, placeholders, "coming soon" sections.

## Motion
- **Everything fades up the same way**, at the same speed, staggered. Use one signature move and quiet entrances.
- **Motion that covers text** (✓) or keeps going with reduced motion (✓).
- **Bounce and pulse everywhere.** Allow one bouncy element per page.
- **A still page** (✓) when the brief asks for life: no feedback on controls, nothing reacts.
