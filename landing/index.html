Questions

Short answers

What does a launch cost?

Solana and pump.fun network fees, plus possibly a platform fee. You see every amount in your wallet before you confirm.

Do you hold my funds?

No. We build a transaction and you sign it in your wallet. Our server has no access to your private key and cannot pay for anything on your behalf.

Do you guarantee profits?

No, and nobody else can. Memecoins are speculation with no intrinsic value. You can lose everything you put in.

Can I take a coin back?

No. What is on the blockchain stays there. That is why you get a preview first and have to confirm twice.

Why was my draft rejected?

You see the reason right away, usually a real name, a third-party brand or a profit promise. Rephrase the idea and try again. Ten drafts per day are possible, and rejected ones count.

Is Slop Mop part of pump.fun?

No. We are an independent project and use the public pump.fun SDK. Your coin lives on pump.fun and follows its rules.
