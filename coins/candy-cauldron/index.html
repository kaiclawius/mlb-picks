How it works

🍬Clusters

5 or more of the same candy touching up, down, left or right pay out. Winning candy pops and new candy falls in. One spin can chain for a long time.

✨Sticky spots

Every square where candy pops gets marked. Pop it again and it turns into a x2 multiplier that doubles every hit, up to x128. Multipliers inside a cluster add up.

🎃Free spins

3 or more trick-or-treat buckets give 10 to 30 free spins. During free spins the multiplier spots never reset, and that’s where the monster wins live.
